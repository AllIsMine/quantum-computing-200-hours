# Quantum Computing: A 200-Hour Learning Journey

A structured, hands-on journey into quantum computing, combining foundational concepts, quantum circuit programming, practical experiments, and portfolio development.

## 🎯 Goal

Build a strong understanding of quantum computing through consistent study, coding, experimentation, and clear technical documentation.

## 📚 Learning Roadmap

- **Foundations:** Qubits, quantum states, superposition, and measurement
- **Quantum Gates:** X, H, Z, S, T, and controlled operations
- **Quantum Circuits:** Circuit construction, simulation, and debugging
- **Entanglement:** Bell states, GHZ states, and measurement statistics
- **Quantum Algorithms:** Quantum oracles, Deutsch–Jozsa, and Bernstein–Vazirani
- **Programming Practice:** Python, Qiskit, and Qiskit Aer
- **Portfolio Development:** Jupyter notebooks, experiment logs, and GitHub documentation
